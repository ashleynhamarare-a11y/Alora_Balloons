Our Story

Beauty in Every
Balloon

Alora Balloons was born from a love of celebration and a belief that every milestone deserves to look and feel extraordinary. Based in the heart of Cape Town, we craft personalised balloon installations that transform spaces into unforgettable memories.

Who We Are

Crafted With Care,
Delivered With Love

At Alora Balloons, we believe that décor is more than just aesthetics — it's about creating an atmosphere that makes people feel something. Whether it's a surprise room setup, a grand event garland, or an intimate table arrangement, every installation is handled with the same level of passion and precision.

We work closely with every client to understand their vision, colour palette, and occasion so that what we create is genuinely tailored to them. No two setups are ever the same.

Serving Cape Town and surrounding areas, Alora Balloons has become a trusted name in event décor — known for quality, reliability, and a personal touch that makes every client feel like a priority.
